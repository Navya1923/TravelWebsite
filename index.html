Est. 1924 — A Century of Wandering

Our Story

It began with a weathered leather journal and a one-way ticket to Marseille. Our founder, Eleanor Whitmore, believed that the truest education was found not in classrooms but in the cobblestone streets of ancient cities, in the quiet monasteries of the Himalayas, and in the bustling night markets of Southeast Asia.

For a century, we have curated journeys that feel less like vacations and more like chapters in a story you'll never forget. Every itinerary is hand-crafted, every guide a storyteller, every destination a page waiting to be written.

— The Wanderer's Chronicle Team
